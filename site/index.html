The improvement loop

A wishlist that earns every item.

Not a feature dump. Not automatic failure scraping. A small feedback system for gaps that are real, reusable, and still unsolved.

  1. 01
    ×

    An existing tool or workaround proves insufficient.

  2. 02
    ◇

    The reusable gap is described without prompts, code, or secrets.

  3. 03
    ≋

    Recurrence, reach, impact, and recency reveal what matters.

  4. 04

    A selected signal becomes a smallest-change experiment with proof and rollback.

  5. 05

    Verified gaps leave the queue. Later regressions remain visible without reopening themselves.

How it works

A control loop,
not an autopilot.

Evidence can recommend work. Only a person can select it, approve an implementation, or retire it.

01 / signal

Observe locally

Collection begins only after consent. Reusable gaps are sanitized, task-deduplicated, and stored locally—never uploaded.

02 / decision

Choose one change

/wishlist next surfaces qualified evidence. Selection prepares a smallest-change card with proof and rollback; it grants no edit authority.

03 / outcome

Verify, then retire

Direct checks and reviewed validators close the loop. New friction stays visible as a review signal without reopening itself.

The goal

Let the environment learn from the work—without taking control from the human.

ZenPi makes friction legible, preserves the evidence, and asks for the smallest intervention that can be proved and reversed.

Use becomes signal. Human judgment turns signal into care. The system gets better without getting noisier.

Built around the loop

Attentive enough to notice.
Explicit enough to trust.

01

Privacy-minimized

Bounded sanitization removes recognizable private patterns. Review local reports before sharing.

02

Evidence-led

Unique tasks and impact outrank novelty, volume, and vague frustration.

03

Human-directed

The wishlist informs change. It never grants itself permission to make it.

The foundation beneath it:

  • focused /zen mode
  • provider-safe delegation
  • isolated browser QA
  • reversible changes
