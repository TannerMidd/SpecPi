Summary

01

No statistically significant solve-rate difference. SpecPi + Jev cost less per attempt than bare Pi in these runs.

How it is measured

02
  • Setup: deepseek-v4.1-flash, one endpoint, Harbor on Docker.
  • Trials: 7 calibration tasks × 5 attempts; 13 widened tasks × 3 (Oh My Pi × 6).
  • Scoring: Harbor rewards and token counts. Costs use the frozen price list. One broken task excluded; one setup timeout unscored.

03
