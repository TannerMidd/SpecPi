Summary

01

No pair of harnesses differs significantly on solve rate in the sittings where both ran. In the sittings where both ran, SpecPi + Jev sent fewer prompt tokens than bare Pi in , by . It cost less in , by about : output tokens are of every harness's bill, so a prompt-token saving moves cost much less than it looks.

One sitting cannot support a claim here. Bare Pi — unchanged software, the same thirteen tasks, the same machine — solved of 39 across launches. That -solve spread is wider than any gap this benchmark has measured between two harnesses.

Every figure below is therefore pooled across sittings, and section 04 shows the per-sitting spread rather than hiding it inside a mean. Comparisons between harnesses use only the sittings where both ran. An earlier reading that set two separate runs side by side appeared to show a SpecPi advantage; it did not survive being repeated.

How it is measured

02
  • Setup: deepseek-v4.1-flash, one endpoint, Harbor on Docker.
  • Trials: 7 calibration tasks × 5 attempts; 13 widened tasks × 3 per sitting (Oh My Pi × 6), repeated across the sittings in section 04.
  • Scoring: Harbor rewards and token counts. Costs use the frozen price list. One broken task excluded; two failed setups unscored.
  • OpenCode and the DeepSeek Harness: one sitting each, run beside Pi and SpecPi + Jev. OpenCode's reasoning tokens are read from its trajectory, because Harbor leaves them out of its output count. The DeepSeek Harness reports no usage, so a proxy inside the container counts what it sends. It is pinned to 0.1.5-rc.3, since the current latest no longer starts. OpenCode's three sqlite-with-gcov attempts stalled after their first minute, waiting on a model response that never closed, and are scored as failures like any other timeout.
  • Builds: every Pi-derived trial reported Pi .
  • Not in the cost: SpecPi + Jev's figure is the agent's own tokens. Harbor does not record the Jev advisor's calls — its own ledger shows — so they are left out rather than estimated. At the advisor's listed per million input tokens, with output free, they are small beside the agent's spend.
