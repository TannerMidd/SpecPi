# RFC-014 — Revised retry budget for northern ingest

Id: RFC-014
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-013

Load testing after the 2026-Q1 capacity review shows the retry budget agreed in RFC-013
is too conservative for the northern ingest path. It is raised to **10 attempts**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-013 is superseded in full.
