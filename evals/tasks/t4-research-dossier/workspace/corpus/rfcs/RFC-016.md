# RFC-016 — Revised fan-out limit for inland warden

Id: RFC-016
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-015

Load testing after the 2025-Q3 capacity review shows the fan-out limit agreed in RFC-015
is too conservative for the inland warden path. It is raised to **7 peers**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-015 is superseded in full.
