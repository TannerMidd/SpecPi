# RFC-008 — Revised retry budget for eastern warden

Id: RFC-008
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-007

Load testing after the 2025-Q3 capacity review shows the retry budget agreed in RFC-007
is too conservative for the eastern warden path. It is raised to **15 attempts**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-007 is superseded in full.
