# RFC-006 — Revised batch ceiling for quiet reaper

Id: RFC-006
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-005

Load testing after the 2026-Q1 capacity review shows the batch ceiling agreed in RFC-005
is too conservative for the quiet reaper path. It is raised to **576 records**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-005 is superseded in full.
