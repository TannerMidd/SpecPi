# RFC-004 — Revised fan-out limit for lower reaper

Id: RFC-004
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-003

Load testing after the 2026-Q1 capacity review shows the fan-out limit agreed in RFC-003
is too conservative for the lower reaper path. It is raised to **4 peers**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-003 is superseded in full.
