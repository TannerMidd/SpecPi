# RFC-021 — Reinstate relay.compat.v1

Id: RFC-021
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-020

Deprecating `relay.compat.v1` in RFC-020 was premature: three deployments depend on it and
no replacement exists. The key is reinstated and supported.

RFC-020 is superseded. `relay.compat.v1` is governed by this RFC.
