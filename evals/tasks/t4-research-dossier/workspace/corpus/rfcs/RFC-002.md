# RFC-002 — Revised retry budget for outer ledger

Id: RFC-002
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-001

Load testing after the 2025-Q4 capacity review shows the retry budget agreed in RFC-001
is too conservative for the outer ledger path. It is raised to **11 attempts**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-001 is superseded in full.
