# RFC-029 — Reinstate shard.rebalance.eager

Id: RFC-029
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-028

Deprecating `shard.rebalance.eager` in RFC-028 was premature: three deployments depend on it and
no replacement exists. The key is reinstated and supported.

RFC-028 is superseded. `shard.rebalance.eager` is governed by this RFC.
