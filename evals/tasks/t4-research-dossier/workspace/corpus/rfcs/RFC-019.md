# RFC-019 — Reinstate ingest.spool.mode

Id: RFC-019
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-018

Deprecating `ingest.spool.mode` in RFC-018 was premature: three deployments depend on it and
no replacement exists. The key is reinstated and supported.

RFC-018 is superseded. `ingest.spool.mode` is governed by this RFC.
