# RFC-027 — Reinstate fanout.peer.pinning

Id: RFC-027
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-026

Deprecating `fanout.peer.pinning` in RFC-026 was premature: three deployments depend on it and
no replacement exists. The key is reinstated and supported.

RFC-026 is superseded. `fanout.peer.pinning` is governed by this RFC.
