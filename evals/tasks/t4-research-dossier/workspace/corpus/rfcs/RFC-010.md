# RFC-010 — Revised fan-out limit for lower sweeper

Id: RFC-010
Status: active
Supersedes: RFC-009

Load testing after the 2025-Q4 capacity review shows the fan-out limit agreed in RFC-009
is too conservative for the lower sweeper path. It is raised to **10 peers**,
effective immediately.

This is the figure in force. RFC-009 is superseded in full.
