# Incident INC-2291

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 69 minutes

## Summary

This review covered the telemetry-archive bucket and the invoice-export bucket. The first of these came through clean.

The second was opened to every tenant in the account rather than its own.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
