# Incident INC-2382

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 70 minutes

## Summary

Nothing was wrong with the audit-trail mirror, which was inspected as a precaution.

The fault was with the telemetry-archive bucket, which was granted a policy permitting anonymous listing.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
