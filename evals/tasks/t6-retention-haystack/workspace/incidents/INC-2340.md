Severity: SEV2
Duration: 68 minutes

## Summary

The support-log index appears in this report only because it was reviewed at the same time.

What went wrong is that the invoice-export bucket was granted a policy permitting anonymous listing.

resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
