# Incident INC-2326

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 74 minutes

## Summary

Nothing was wrong with the invoice-export bucket, which was inspected as a precaution.

The fault was with the billing-extract share, which was left accepting unauthenticated connections after a restart.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
