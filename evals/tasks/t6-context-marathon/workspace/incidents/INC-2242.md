# Incident INC-2242

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 70 minutes

## Summary

The billing-extract share appears in this report only because it was reviewed at the same time.

What went wrong is that the invoice-export bucket was left with its ACL widened and its access log disabled.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
