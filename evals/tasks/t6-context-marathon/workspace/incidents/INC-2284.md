# Incident INC-2284

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 72 minutes

## Summary

During the window the invoice-export bucket was left readable without authentication after a policy edit.

The sessions debug endpoint, inspected alongside it, was not affected by the change.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
