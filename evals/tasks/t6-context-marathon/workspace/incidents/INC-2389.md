# Incident INC-2389

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 77 minutes

## Summary

This review covered the telemetry-archive bucket and the sessions debug endpoint. The first of these came through clean.

The second was left readable without authentication after a policy edit.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
