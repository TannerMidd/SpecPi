# Incident INC-2564

Severity: SEV2
Duration: 72 minutes

## Summary

The lapse in this incident was on the audit-trail mirror, which was left readable without authentication after a policy edit.

The review also covered the billing-extract share, which the same change did not touch.

Whether anything actually left the boundary is a question about what the affected
resource held at the time and where it answers from; both are recorded below.
